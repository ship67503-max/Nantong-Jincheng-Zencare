# JCZCARE SEO Content Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap is the long-term editorial map for JCZCARE's B2B organic growth. It targets brands, importers, distributors, retailers and purchasing teams sourcing OEM, ODM and private-label pet absorbent products. Each topic is designed to answer a distinct buyer question and lead naturally to a factory, product, customization or inquiry page.

## Editorial Rules

- Publish one focused page per topic; do not merge topics that serve different search intents.
- Use first-hand manufacturing detail only where JCZCARE can verify it from factory records, approved specifications or real production material.
- Keep commercial claims cautious and specific. Never invent certifications, capacity, customer names, test results or delivery guarantees.
- Link each article to a relevant factory page, customization route, product page, quality page, contact route and at least three related articles.
- Review search performance in Google Search Console before expanding a cluster or creating close variants.
- Refresh material, packaging, import and regulatory guidance when market requirements change.
