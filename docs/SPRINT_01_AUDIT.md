# Sprint 01 Audit

## Audit Purpose
Sprint 01 is the baseline audit for the JCZCARE website. The goal is to identify technical, SEO, performance, content, trust, and conversion issues before making additional improvements.

This file is a checklist and working audit document. Mark each item as:

- Not Started
- In Progress
- Passed
- Needs Work
- Blocked

## 1. SEO Checklist
- Confirm all public pages have unique meta titles.
- Confirm all public pages have unique meta descriptions.
- Confirm every important page has one clear H1.
- Confirm page titles match buyer search intent.
- Confirm content targets B2B OEM/ODM intent.
- Confirm no important page has thin content.
- Confirm no duplicate pages compete for the same keyword.
- Confirm internal links support priority pages.
- Confirm sitemap includes all public pages.
- Confirm robots.txt allows search engines to crawl public pages.

## 2. Technical SEO Checklist
- Confirm `/sitemap.xml` returns XML, not HTML.
- Confirm sitemap Content-Type is `application/xml`.
- Confirm `/robots.txt` returns plain text.
- Confirm canonical URLs use HTTPS absolute URLs.
- Confirm Vercel rewrites do not intercept static SEO files.
- Confirm 404/private/admin/development routes are excluded from sitemap.
- Confirm Google Search Console verification tag is present.
- Confirm no duplicate canonical tags.
- Confirm no duplicate meta description tags.
- Confirm no duplicate Open Graph tags.

## 3. Performance Checklist
- Review JavaScript bundle size.
- Review large media files.
- Confirm videos are compressed or externally hosted.
- Confirm images are properly sized.
- Confirm lazy loading for non-critical images.
- Confirm no unnecessary dependencies were added.
- Confirm build output does not include raw source media.

## 4. Core Web Vitals Checklist
- Measure Largest Contentful Paint.
- Measure Interaction to Next Paint.
- Measure Cumulative Layout Shift.
- Review hero video impact on LCP.
- Review font loading behavior.
- Review image dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Review mobile rendering speed.

## 5. Schema Checklist
- Confirm Organization schema exists.
- Confirm WebSite schema exists.
- Confirm Breadcrumb schema exists for important pages.
- Confirm FAQ schema appears only where FAQ content exists.
- Validate JSON-LD format.
- Confirm schema content matches visible page content.
- Confirm phone, email, website, and company name are correct.

## 6. Metadata Checklist
- Review homepage title and description.
- Review product page titles and descriptions.
- Review SEO landing page titles and descriptions.
- Review news/blog page titles and descriptions.
- Review Open Graph image availability.
- Review Twitter Card output.
- Confirm image URLs are absolute where needed.

## 7. Factory Trust Checklist
- Confirm factory name is consistent.
- Confirm source factory positioning is clear.
- Confirm factory images are visible and credible.
- Confirm production capability is explained.
- Confirm quality control is explained.
- Confirm OEM/ODM workflow is explained.
- Confirm contact details are accurate.
- Confirm trust claims are professional and supportable.

## 8. Landing Pages Checklist
- Review commercial SEO landing pages.
- Confirm each page has a unique search intent.
- Confirm each page includes internal links.
- Confirm each page includes a relevant CTA.
- Confirm each page has enough buyer-focused content.
- Confirm pages do not feel duplicated or auto-generated.

## 9. Blog Checklist
- Confirm blog page exists.
- Confirm article pages have unique URLs.
- Confirm articles support buyer education.
- Confirm articles link to product or inquiry pages.
- Confirm blog content avoids unsupported claims.
- Confirm blog pages are included in sitemap.

## 10. Inquiry System Checklist
- Confirm inquiry form fields are clear.
- Confirm product-specific request pages work.
- Confirm form labels are understandable.
- Confirm required fields are sensible.
- Confirm submission flow is documented.
- Confirm backend or record-keeping plan exists.
- Confirm WhatsApp contact path is correct.

## 11. CTA Checklist
- Confirm CTAs are visible.
- Confirm CTA text is B2B relevant.
- Confirm product pages lead to request forms.
- Confirm contact page has clear next steps.
- Confirm WhatsApp and email actions are easy to access.
- Confirm CTAs are not excessive or distracting.

## 12. Google EEAT Checklist
- Confirm company identity is clear.
- Confirm factory expertise is demonstrated.
- Confirm content is practical and accurate.
- Confirm contact information is visible.
- Confirm business pages support trust.
- Confirm claims are not exaggerated.
- Confirm helpful content exists beyond sales copy.

## 13. Accessibility Checklist
- Confirm text contrast is readable.
- Confirm links have descriptive labels.
- Confirm buttons have clear text.
- Confirm images have alt text.
- Confirm forms have labels.
- Confirm keyboard navigation is possible.
- Confirm motion does not block access to content.

## 14. Responsive Design Checklist
- Review desktop layout.
- Review laptop layout.
- Review tablet layout.
- Review mobile layout.
- Confirm text does not overflow.
- Confirm cards and grids stack properly.
- Confirm navigation remains usable.
- Confirm forms remain usable.

## 15. Internal Linking Checklist
- Confirm homepage links to major sections.
- Confirm product cards link to product detail pages.
- Confirm product pages link to inquiry pages.
- Confirm SEO pages link to related pages.
- Confirm blog posts link to relevant product or SEO pages.
- Confirm footer links are useful and crawlable.

## 16. Image Optimization Checklist
- Confirm image filenames are descriptive.
- Confirm image alt text is meaningful.
- Confirm large images are compressed.
- Confirm non-critical images load lazily.
- Confirm product images match product type.
- Confirm factory images support trust.
- Confirm no broken image paths exist.

## 17. Conversion Rate Checklist
- Review buyer journey from homepage to inquiry.
- Review buyer journey from product page to inquiry.
- Review buyer journey from SEO page to inquiry.
- Review CTA placement.
- Review form friction.
- Review trust signals near conversion points.
- Review whether buyers understand what to do next.
