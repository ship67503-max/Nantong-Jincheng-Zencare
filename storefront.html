BEFORE WE GET STARTED

A few good
questions.

A clearer brief makes a better beginning.

What can I customize?

Discuss product dimensions, materials, absorbency targets, functional options, pack counts and private-label packaging. Available options depend on the product you select.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ is fixed. Ask our team for the applicable quantity through an inquiry or WhatsApp.

Can I review samples before ordering?

Yes. Start with a product brief so sample specifications, preparation time and shipping arrangements can be confirmed for your project.

What do you need for a quotation?

Please include the product category, size, estimated quantity, target market and packaging requirements. Reference samples or specification sheets can help clarify your needs.

How is the production lead time confirmed?

Timing is reviewed after the specifications, packaging and quantity are agreed. Sample approval, material availability and the production schedule all affect the final plan.
